# Figure 16: Pearson correlations of condition-level mean annoyance

Panels a/b/c correspond to drone1/drone2/violin. The `*_pearson.csv` matrices contain exact coefficients extracted from the saved heatmap files. They were independently reproduced from `*_condition_means.csv`, which contains 9, 9 and 5 conditions respectively. Model order is PAZ (Zwicker and Fastl), PAM (More), PAD (Di et al.). These are correlations across operating-condition means, not across all time samples. The condition means come from Figures 13–15. See `../methods/PA_correlations.m`.
